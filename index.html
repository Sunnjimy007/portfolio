Fintech · Trade Intelligence · Claude Code

InsiderIQ — Politician & Insider Trade Intelligence Dashboard

US politicians and corporate insiders are required to disclose their stock trades — but that data is scattered across government and third-party sites, and not every disclosure is equal. I built InsiderIQ to aggregate congressional trading disclosures and SEC insider filings into one dashboard, score every trade across seven weighted dimensions (recency, filing speed, trade size, cluster buying, committee relevance, and more), and surface the highest-conviction signals for one-click paper trading via Alpaca.

How might we turn scattered, delayed disclosure data into a ranked, actionable signal — separating a junior congressperson's routine $1,500 ETF buy from a senator's $500,000 stock purchase right before their committee votes on it?

Family Trivia Night — Live Scoring & Host App

My 9-year-old loves running family trivia nights — but the experience kept breaking down at the coordination layer. Attention drifted between questions, scores got disputed, and the host ended up managing logistics instead of enjoying the game. I applied the same 0→1 product thinking I use professionally: identify the friction, define the problem, ship a focused solution. The result is a trivia host app that handles timing, live scoring, and game flow — so the night stays energetic from the first question to the final leaderboard.

How might we help a family run an energetic, in-person trivia game where players stay engaged and scores are captured accurately, without adding friction or forcing everyone to stop and manage logistics?

Running · South East Asia · Claude Code

Racefinder — Running Event Discovery for South East Asia

As a runner based in Singapore, I kept hitting the same problem: there's no single trusted source to discover running events. Races get missed, early-bird windows close, and relevant events don't surface until registration is full. I built Racefinder to fix this — launching first in Singapore and Australia as the MVP, with plans to expand across South East Asia.

Today, runners bounce between a dozen fragmented websites to piece together race options — missing early-bird deadlines and relevant events in the process. Racefinder starts in Singapore and Australia, with SEA expansion to follow.
